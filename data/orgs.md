## Tools & Services

### CFFLA Gun Law Bot
- URL: https://www.cffla.org/gun-laws
- Description: The Gun Laws of Colorado page is CFFLA's free public search tool for Colorado firearms law, letting users look up specific bills, ask plain-language questions, or browse bills, statutes, and rules organized by category. It is part of the Colorado Federal Firearms Licensees Association's public resources, which include GunLawBot, a chat assistant that answers gun-law questions with citations to the underlying law.
- CO contact: CFFLA, Colorado office (Eastlake, CO) — info@cffla.org

- URL: https://co2aportal.com/
