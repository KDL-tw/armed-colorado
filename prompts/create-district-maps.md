# Improve The "Find You Colorado Legislators" Page

## MISSION
Make the "Find You Colorado Legislators" page of the Armed Colorado website more informative and better looking by including a link to the official Colorado gov app and county maps that show House and Senate legislator districts.

## WEBPAGE FORMAT
The structure of the update will have the following format:
- Link to the official "Find my Legislator" app
- How districts are determined
- House districts with a county map
- Senate districts with a county map

## WEBPAGE CONTENT
### Official App
Leave the link to the official Colorado state government "Find your legislator" app there.

### How Districts are Determined
This section needs to have information that is cited to official colorado state sources on:
1) How many districts there are in Colorado.
2) How districts for each chamber (house and senate) are determined
3) How often districts can be redrawn and how those redraws happen.

### House Districts
This section should have a large map of the Colorado state house districts. It should be a map of Colorado with delimiters marking the counties underneath and ontop should be a color version of the most recent drawn state house districts and the district number. A good example can be found here: https://www.kunc.org/politics/2021-11-15/colorado-has-new-legislative-districts-find-out-which-one-is-yours. The popout of the Denver Metro Area and the Colorado Springs Metro Area must be included as well. Don't copy the the picture in the URL because it could be out of date. You'll need to find an image of the most recent districts and use that. Use a color scheme that makes things easy to read and understand.

### Senate Districts
This section should have a large map of the Colorado state Senate districts. It should be a map of Colorado with delimiters marking the counties underneath and ontop should be a color version of the most recent drawn state senate districts and the district number. A popout of the Denver Metro Area and the Colorado Springs Metro Area must be included as well. Use a color scheme that makes things easy to read and understand.

## UPDATING THE MAPS
Based off the information you find for the "How Districts are Determined" section, create some sort of job that will accurately fetch and create the new state house and senate district maps for when they get redrawn the next time. I want these maps automatically updated.
