### Webpage Content
The subsections below describe the content that should go into each section of the webpage. Use this page as a reference to help create content: 

#### Header Title
- change "Overview pointers only — statutes and sheriff policies change." to something more descriptive of the entire webpage.

#### Concealed Handgun Carry Laws
This section should broadly describe the law associated with concealing a handgun in Colorado. It does not need to include the laws associated with obtaining or renewing a CHP as that will be covered in proceeding subsections. It should cover:
- Where exactly in the Colorado Revised Statutes the laws are listed
	- Hyperlinks to each section should be made.
- Where one is legally allowed to carry a handgun concealed with a permit
- Where one is not legally allowed to carry a handgun concealed with a permit
- Reciprocity laws and agreements Colorado has with other states
- What to do if your CHP is lost or stolen
- Any exemptions for a CHP and how to apply for them
- Travelling with your CHP
- Duties to announce to an office if you have a CHP
- Brandishing laws
- Castle doctrine and stand your ground laws

For each one of the subsections above, a general summary of the actual relevant law must be included, as well as a hyperlink to the CRS for the exact statute or statutes mentioned. You may include or create graphics and insert them wherever necessary to convey the message if you think it is better suited. 

#### Obtaining a Concealed Handgun Permit (CHP) in Colorado

Let the graphic depict only the positive case (i.e. no exceptions or exemptions). In a follow on subsection, include any exceptions or exemptions. Include fees, restrictions, expiration periods, prohibited persons, costs, etc.

In another subsection, include common FAQs for the process and the permit.

Each aspect of this section must be properly cited to CRS.

#### CHP Reciprocity
This section should:
- Define clearly what reciprocity means and give an example.
- Create an interactive map of the USA that uses the following color scheme:
	- Color a state green if they have full reciprocity with Colorado
	- Color a state red if they have no reciprocity with Colorado
	- Color a state yellow if they have any other state of reciprocity and explain what the discrepancy is. 

Each aspect of this section must be properly cited to CRS.

#### Renewing Your CHP in Colorado
Create an infographic that describes the details for how one goes about renewing their CHP in Colorado for the first time and subsequent times. Let the graphic depict only the positive case (i.e. no exceptions or exemptions). In a follow on subsection, include any exceptions or exemptions. Include fees, restrictions, expiration periods, prohibited persons, costs, etc.

Each aspect of this section must be properly cited to CRS.

#### Contacting your Sheriff Department
In this section, an interactive map of Calorado showing its counties should be drawn. The county names should be clearly labelled and easy for the user to read. When the user hovers over the the county, a popup should show that displays:
- The name of the sheriff department for that county
- The name of the current sherrif
- a hyperlink to that county's sheriff's webpage
- the contact email address for that sheriff department that is relevant for CHP applications
- a hyperlink to that sheriff department's CHP application
- the phone number for that sheriff department.
- Hours of operation for that sheriff department.

When a user clicks a county on the map, it should take them to that county's CHP webpage.
